description=An add-on for [b]Dazed Dank[/b]. With it, Dank's grow bags, DWC buckets, RDWC sites and flood tables take vanilla crops as well as cannabis. Tomatoes, lettuce, herbs and the rest grow by vanilla's rules: water them by hand in a bag, or let the reservoir water them in hydro, where they grow a little faster.

description=[tr][td][b]Game version[/b][/td][td]Build 42 only (42.0+)[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]Requires[/b][/td][td][b]Dazed Dank[/b] 0.56 or later (subscribe to it too)[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]Multiplayer[/b][/td][td]Yes. The server and every player need it[/td][/tr]

description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/GardenCrops/images/h-where.png[/img]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/GardenCrops/images/where.png[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*]Every vanilla crop, 55 in all, grows in a [b]Small, Large or XL Grow Bag[/b]. Fill the bag with potting soil first, as for cannabis.
description=[*]Hydro takes 37 of them: herbs, leafy greens, tomatoes, peppers, strawberries, flowers and more. That means [b]DWC and XL DWC buckets[/b], [b]RDWC sites[/b] and [b]flood tables[/b]. As for cannabis, put the rockwool or clay pebbles in and fill the reservoir before you sow.
description=[*]Root, bulb, sprawling and grain crops need soil, so hydro refuses them: potatoes, sweet potatoes, carrots, radishes, sugar beets, turnips, onions, garlic, wild garlic, leeks, pumpkins, watermelons, corn, zucchini, wheat, barley, rye and flax. In hydro their row is greyed out with [i]This crop needs soil: use a grow bag[/i].
description=[*][b]Sow Seed[/b] on a container lists the seeds you carry that can grow there.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/GardenCrops/images/h-grow.png[/img]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/GardenCrops/images/grow.png[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*]Crops grow by vanilla's own rules. Dazed Dank does the growing; this add-on says which crops go where and supplies the plants.
description=[*]In a grow bag you water by hand, like a vanilla plot.
description=[*]In hydro the plant drinks from the reservoir, about 0.3 L an hour. It stays watered while the reservoir holds water (on a flood table, while the rockwool is wet) and dries out when it runs dry.
description=[*]In hydro each growth step takes 15% less time.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/GardenCrops/images/h-harvest.png[/img]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/GardenCrops/images/indoors.png[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*]Harvesting a one-time crop leaves the container empty and ready to sow again. Crops that regrow keep going after you pick them, as they do in a vanilla plot. A plant that rots away empties the container too.
description=[*]With vanilla's [b]Kill Crops Grown Inside[/b] sandbox option on, a powered grow lamp that reaches the plant, or a Dank grow room, keeps it alive indoors.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/GardenCrops/images/h-plants.png[/img]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/GardenCrops/images/plants.png[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*]Vanilla paints the tilled furrow into every crop sprite. Here each crop is drawn without its soil, as one plant that stands in the container.
description=[*]Every growth stage is there, with its sick, dying and dead looks too.
description=[*]The plants are cut from Project Zomboid's own crop art.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/GardenCrops/images/h-compat.png[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*]Build 42 only. Needs [b]Dazed Dank[/b] 0.56 or later.
description=[*]Without this add-on, Dank's containers take cannabis only. Cannabis grows the same either way.
description=[*]Vanilla crops only. Crops from other mods have no plant here and can't go in Dank's containers.
description=[/list]
